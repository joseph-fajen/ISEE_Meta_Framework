# Wisdom Extracted

## On Evaluation and Benchmarking

**"I don't like the numbers... I'm beginning to lose trust in the leaderboards as well"**
- Traditional metrics and benchmarks are becoming unreliable as evaluation methods
- Personal, practical testing often reveals more about model capabilities than formal benchmarks
- Simple, creative tests can be more revealing than complex standardized evaluations

**The Pelican Principle**
- Testing impossible tasks reveals model creativity and problem-solving approaches
- Models show their "personality" and reasoning process through comments and approach
- What matters isn't perfection, but how systems attempt to solve unsolvable problems

## On Technological Progress

**"I never thought that was going to happen... I thought that was impossible"**
- Technology progression often exceeds our expectations and assumptions
- What seems impossible today may become routine within months
- The pace of change in AI makes long-term predictions increasingly unreliable

**Efficiency Over Scale**
- DeepSeek proved that better training methods matter more than larger budgets
- Sometimes constraints (like Chinese GPU restrictions) force more innovative solutions
- Efficiency improvements can democratize access to previously exclusive capabilities

## On Practical Development

**Local Models Renaissance**
- "If you lost interest in local models... it's worth paying attention to them again"
- Technologies that were once dismissed can become valuable as they mature
- Personal computational power for serious AI work is now viable

**The Power of Combination**
- Tools + Reasoning = "the most powerful technique in all of AI engineering right now"
- Individual capabilities become exponentially more powerful when combined
- Iterative problem-solving (search → reason → refine → repeat) unlocks new possibilities

## On System Behavior and Control

**The Sycophancy Lesson**
- "The cure to sycophantic is you tell the bot don't be sycophantic"
- Simple, direct instructions often solve complex behavioral problems
- System prompts are powerful tools for controlling AI behavior

**Loss of Control Warning**
- Features like ChatGPT memory can compromise user control over context
- As AI systems become more autonomous, maintaining human agency becomes critical
- Power users need explicit control over all inputs and context

## On Security and Trust

**The Lethal Trifecta**
- Private data + malicious instructions + exfiltration tools = significant risk
- AI systems will act on their values when given the tools and permission
- Security requires understanding how AI systems interpret instructions about ethics

## On Evaluation Philosophy

**Personal Benchmarks Matter**
- Create evaluation methods that match your specific use cases
- Crowd-sourced evaluations (like ELO rankings) can be more meaningful than lab benchmarks
- Practical, domain-specific testing often reveals more than general capability scores

## On Market Dynamics

**Price vs. Performance Reality**
- Being 750x more expensive doesn't mean being 750x better
- Market pricing often doesn't reflect actual capability differences
- Competition drives dramatic cost reductions while maintaining quality

## Life Lessons

1. **Question established metrics** - What everyone accepts as truth may be inadequate
2. **Test personally** - Your specific needs may not align with general benchmarks  
3. **Embrace constraints** - Limitations often drive better solutions than unlimited resources
4. **Stay alert to resurgence** - Previously dismissed technologies can become valuable
5. **Maintain control** - Convenience features that remove user agency should be carefully considered
6. **Expect the impossible** - Technological progress regularly exceeds conservative expectations
