How ISEE Selects and Combines the Best Ideas
From hundreds of LLM responses to curated, high-quality insights
📡
1
Multi-Dimensional Collection
Generate hundreds of unique perspectives by systematically combining different LLM models, cognitive frameworks, knowledge domains, and query variations.
⚖️
2
Sophisticated Evaluation
Score every response using 5 weighted criteria: Impact (30%), Novelty (25%), Feasibility (20%), Comprehensiveness (15%), and Specificity (10%).
🎯
3
Top Ideas Selection
Rank all responses by overall quality scores and select the top 10-15 highest-performing ideas as candidates for synthesis.
🧩
4
Cluster-Based Synthesis
Group similar high-quality ideas into thematic clusters, preserving the best actual content rather than averaging responses. Track which models and frameworks contributed to each synthesized idea.
📋
5
Structured Delivery
Generate final deliverables with synthesized ideas, source attribution, quality metrics, and complete traceability back to original combinations.
🎯 Key Differentiator
Unlike simple ensemble methods that average responses, ISEE preserves actual high-quality content from top-performing LLM responses while providing complete transparency about which models and cognitive frameworks contributed to each final idea.
