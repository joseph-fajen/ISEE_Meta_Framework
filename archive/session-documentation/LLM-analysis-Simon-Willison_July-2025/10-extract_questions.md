# Key Questions Raised and Worth Exploring

## Fundamental Questions About AI Progress

**How do we reliably evaluate AI model quality when traditional benchmarks are failing?**
- What makes a good evaluation methodology in rapidly evolving AI landscape?
- Should we rely more on community-driven assessment than formal benchmarks?
- How do we balance objective metrics with subjective quality assessment?

**What are the real training costs for world-class AI models?**
- Is DeepSeek's $5.5M training cost for V3 representative or exceptional?
- How much can training efficiency improve beyond current methods?
- What are the true minimum resources needed for frontier-class AI?

**How much further can parameter compression go while maintaining capabilities?**
- Is there a theoretical limit to parameter efficiency improvements?
- Can we achieve GPT-4 performance in sub-20B parameter models?
- What new architectures might enable even greater efficiency?

## Market and Economic Questions

**Why do premium AI pricing models consistently fail in the market?**
- What value proposition would justify 750x higher pricing?
- How should AI companies price genuinely novel capabilities?
- When does the market accept premium pricing vs. commodity pricing?

**How will open source releases continue to disrupt closed AI systems?**
- What business models remain viable when capabilities become open?
- How do closed providers maintain competitive advantages?
- Will open source eventually dominate all AI capabilities?

**What are the long-term economic implications of AI capability democratization?**
- How will industries change when individual developers have frontier AI access?
- What happens to competitive moats based on AI capability?
- How will employment and productivity be affected by widespread AI access?

## Technical Capability Questions

**How far can local AI capabilities advance on consumer hardware?**
- What are the physical limits of local model execution?
- How will specialized consumer AI hardware change possibilities?
- Can local models ever fully replace cloud-based AI for all use cases?

**What new capabilities emerge from combining reasoning with tools?**
- What types of problems become solvable with iterative AI problem-solving?
- How do we design optimal workflows for reasoning + tools combinations?
- What are the scaling properties of AI systems that can improve their own tool usage?

**How do we maintain human control as AI systems become more autonomous?**
- What level of AI autonomy is beneficial vs. problematic?
- How do we balance convenience with user agency in AI systems?
- What safeguards prevent AI systems from taking unintended actions?

## Security and Safety Questions

**How serious is the "lethal trifecta" security risk in practice?**
- What real-world scenarios could exploit private data + malicious instructions + exfiltration?
- How do we defend against sophisticated prompt injection attacks?
- What security frameworks are needed for tool-enabled AI systems?

**Should AI systems autonomously report perceived wrongdoing?**
- Under what circumstances is AI "snitching" beneficial vs. harmful?
- How do we ensure AI systems don't misinterpret situations when taking autonomous action?
- What ethical frameworks should guide AI decision-making about reporting?

**How do we prevent AI systems from being manipulated through values-based prompting?**
- What constitutes legitimate vs. malicious ethical instruction to AI?
- How do we distinguish between appropriate and inappropriate autonomous AI action?
- What safeguards prevent social engineering of AI systems through ethical appeals?

## Evaluation and Quality Assessment

**What makes Simon Willison's pelican benchmark effective when formal benchmarks fail?**
- Why do impossible tasks reveal more about AI capabilities than solvable ones?
- How do we design evaluation methods that remain reliable as AI improves?
- What role should community-generated evaluation play vs. academic benchmarks?

**How do we assess subjective qualities like creativity and artistic capability in AI?**
- Can community consensus (like ELO rankings) provide reliable quality assessment?
- How do we evaluate AI outputs that have no objective "correct" answer?
- What criteria should guide evaluation of AI creativity and problem-solving approach?

**Why are traditional AI benchmarks becoming less trustworthy?**
- Is benchmark gaming inevitable as AI systems improve?
- How do we create evaluation methods that resist gaming?
- What new evaluation paradigms might replace traditional benchmarks?

## Innovation and Development Questions

**Why do resource constraints seem to drive better AI innovations than unlimited resources?**
- What psychological or organizational factors make constraints beneficial for innovation?
- How can well-funded organizations replicate constraint-driven innovation benefits?
- What types of constraints are most effective for driving breakthrough innovations?

**How do we predict which AI technologies will mature from "rubbish" to valuable?**
- What indicators suggest a technology is worth revisiting after initial dismissal?
- How frequently should we reassess previously unsuccessful approaches?
- What patterns distinguish temporary limitations from fundamental flaws?

**What determines the success or failure of AI product launches?**
- Why did ChatGPT Vision succeed massively while GPT-4.5 failed quickly?
- What factors make AI products go viral vs. get ignored?
- How important is pricing vs. capability vs. user experience in AI product success?

- What new forms of inequality might emerge in an AI-democratized world?
- How will creative industries change when powerful AI tools are universally accessible?

**How do we maintain meaningful human agency in an AI-integrated world?**
- What tasks should remain exclusively human vs. AI-assisted vs. fully automated?
- How do we preserve human skill development when AI can handle most cognitive tasks?

**What are the geopolitical implications of AI capability democratization?**

- What happens to technology export controls when AI models can be trained everywhere?
- How do nations maintain competitive advantages when AI capabilities become commoditized?

- What UI/UX patterns best balance automation with explicit user control?
- How do we make complex AI capabilities accessible to non-technical users?
- What defaults and settings give users appropriate control over AI behavior?
